# EEOC / voluntary self-identification dropdown vocabulary

US federal EEOC forms follow a small set of standardized categories but employers render them with slightly different labels. These are the standard options; append variants as you see them.

---

## Gender

Canonical options:
- Male
- Female
- Non-binary
- Decline to self-identify / Prefer not to say

Variants seen: `["Male", "Man", "M", "Female", "Woman", "F", "Non-binary", "Nonbinary", "Non-Binary", "Other", "Prefer not to say", "Decline to self-identify", "I do not wish to self-identify", "Decline to answer"]`

## Hispanic or Latino (yes/no question, asked separately from race on US forms)

Canonical: Yes / No / Decline

Variants: `["Yes", "Hispanic or Latino", "No", "Not Hispanic or Latino", "Prefer not to say", "Decline to self-identify"]`

## Race / Ethnicity (non-Hispanic classification)

Canonical options (EEO-1 categories):
- White
- Black or African American
- Asian
- Native Hawaiian or Other Pacific Islander
- American Indian or Alaska Native
- Two or More Races
- Decline to self-identify

Variants seen: `["White", "Caucasian", "Black or African American", "Black", "African American", "Asian", "Asian American", "Native Hawaiian or Other Pacific Islander", "Pacific Islander", "American Indian or Alaska Native", "Native American", "Two or More Races", "Multiracial", "Mixed", "Other", "Prefer not to say", "Decline to self-identify"]`

## Veteran status

Canonical: Not a veteran / Protected veteran / Decline

Variants: `["I am not a protected veteran", "Not a veteran", "No", "I am a protected veteran", "Protected veteran", "Yes", "I identify as one or more of the classifications of protected veteran", "I don't wish to answer", "Prefer not to say", "Decline to self-identify"]`

## Disability

Canonical: Yes / No / Decline (OFCCP Form CC-305 exact wording)

Variants: `["Yes, I have a disability, or have had one in the past", "Yes", "No, I do not have a disability and have not had one in the past", "No", "I do not want to answer", "Prefer not to say", "I don't wish to answer"]`

## Transgender (sometimes asked separately)

Variants: `["Yes", "No", "Prefer not to say", "Decline to self-identify"]`
