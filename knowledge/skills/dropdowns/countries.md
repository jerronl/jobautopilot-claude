# Country dropdown vocabulary

Country names vary across ATS — "United States" / "United States of America" / "USA" / "U.S." / "US" are all common. Also the phone country code dropdowns usually prefix with the dial code ("United States of America (+1)").

Append new variants you encounter.

---

## United States

Variants: `["United States", "United States of America", "USA", "U.S.", "U.S.A.", "US", "America", "United States of America (+1)", "United States (+1)", "US (+1)"]`

Note on Workday phone-country-code widget: the dial-code form "United States of America (+1)" is selected via the same multiselect drill-down as other source fields (see `ats_playbooks/workday.md`).

## Canada

Variants: `["Canada", "CA", "Canada (+1)"]`

## United Kingdom

Variants: `["United Kingdom", "UK", "U.K.", "Great Britain", "Britain", "England", "United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland", "United Kingdom (+44)"]`

## China

Variants: `["China", "People's Republic of China", "PRC", "Mainland China", "CN", "China (+86)"]`
